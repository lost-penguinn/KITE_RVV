KITE_RVV – Work Summary

Work completed:

1.  Added floating-point vector minimum and maximum instructions:
    -   vfmin.vv
    -   vfmax.vv
2.  Added floating-point vector sign-injection instructions:
    -   vfsgnj.vv
    -   vfsgnjn.vv
    -   vfsgnjx.vv

How the instructions were implemented: - Added the instruction names to
the instruction list and assembler syntax mapping. - Added their
machine-code generation mappings in src/assembler/code_generator.cpp. -
Added instruction enum entries and decoder cases in
src/processor/rvss/rvss_processor.cpp. - Added SEW=32 validation and
vector execution handling. - Reused the existing scalar ALU operations:
FMIN_S and FMAX_S for vfmin.vv and vfmax.vv. FSGNJ_S, FSGNJN_S and
FSGNJX_S for the three sign-injection instructions. - No changes were
needed in alu.cpp or alu.h.

Testing: - Tested vfmin.vv and vfmax.vv with ordinary positive and
negative floating-point values. - Tested signed-zero behavior: vfmin
selects -0.0 and vfmax selects +0.0 when comparing +0.0 and -0.0. -
Tested quiet-NaN cases. - Tested vfsgnj.vv, vfsgnjn.vv and vfsgnjx.vv
with positive and negative FP32 values, and checked the resulting
vector-register bit patterns. - The tests produced the expected results.

Other work discussed: - Inspected the existing scalar ALU support for
FCLASS_S and FSQRT_S while considering vfclass.v and vfsqrt.v. - Decided
to skip vfclass.v and vfsqrt.v for now; these were inspected but not
implemented.

Summary: Implemented and tested five additional FP32 vector
instructions: vfmin.vv, vfmax.vv, vfsgnj.vv, vfsgnjn.vv and vfsgnjx.vv.
The implementation reused existing scalar ALU operations and added the
required assembler, encoding, decoding and vector execution support.
